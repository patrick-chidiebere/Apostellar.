### Patrick Chidiebere David

Developer, UI/UX Designer, Writer, and Founder of APOSTELLAR

My starting point is Lagos. My ambition is to build technology that matters.

I am Patrick Chidiebere David, a young developer, UI/UX designer, and writer from Lagos, Nigeria, building toward a future in computer science. I founded APOSTELLAR, a digital and technology studio guided by a simple vision: Creating What's Next. My journey is driven by curiosity, creativity, and the belief that where an idea begins should never determine how far it can go.

I care about more than how a digital product looks. I want to understand the problems it solves, the people it serves, and how technology can transform ideas into meaningful experiences. My interests include web development, UI/UX design, and exploring how emerging technologies can address practical challenges. I am still learning, experimenting, and developing the skills to turn ambitious ideas into useful digital products.

Through APOSTELLAR, I am building a foundation for my work in technology. One of the projects I am developing is Cortex AI, an educational technology concept exploring the possibility of an AI-powered tutor for Nigerian students preparing for examinations such as JAMB and WASSCE. The goal is to investigate how accessible technology could support students in their learning. Cortex AI remains a work in progress, representing both my interest in artificial intelligence and my desire to explore solutions to problems that matter.

I have also written about this ambition and the realities of building as a young developer with limited resources in my Crowdpen article, [Programming the Future: Why $200 Is More Than Money for Cortex AI](https://www.crowdpen.co/%40Patrick/stories/IfIHad%24200/programming-the-future-why-200-is-more-than-money-for-cortex-ai). For me, the experience of building is about more than the finished product. It is about learning to work with what is available, questioning what is possible, and continuing to make progress even when the resources are limited.

Writing has become another important part of my journey. Through essay competitions, I have explored ideas, developed my ability to communicate, and earned recognition for my work. I placed third in the National Travel Essay Competition in 2024, followed by second-place finishes in 2025 and 2026. In 2025, I also placed second in the Caritas Nigeria @ 15 Essay Competition. An excerpt of my 2025 essay on “Leadership Matters” was featured in the Kemper Human Rights Education Foundation (KHREF) essay excerpts.

In 2026, I received the inaugural Steadfast Spirit Award from the National Travel Essay Competition, a recognition associated with perseverance and continuing to try despite previous setbacks. Its meaning goes beyond a certificate or title. It reflects a principle I want to carry into everything I do: progress does not always arrive immediately, and the willingness to keep learning matters.

Beyond technology and writing, chess has also been part of my development. As a member of my school's chess team, I participated in the 2024 NNPC–SNEPCo Chess Championship, where our team placed third, and the 2024 Ecobank National Chess Competition, where our team finished eighth. Chess has given me another way to practise strategic thinking, patience, and learning from decisions.

My journey is still unfolding. I am not presenting myself as a finished expert or claiming to have achieved everything I hope to accomplish. I am a young builder learning through projects, writing, competition, and experience. There are skills I still want to master, products I hope to bring to life, and problems I want to understand more deeply. Each project, challenge, and opportunity to learn contributes to the person and computer scientist I aspire to become.

I want to build technology that is not only impressive but useful; products that consider the people who will use them; and ideas that can grow beyond the circumstances in which they began. I want to keep documenting the journey honestly, sharing what I learn, and creating work that speaks for itself.

I am Patrick Chidiebere David. A developer, a designer, a writer, a chess player, and a founder. I am still learning, still growing, and still discovering what is possible.

I am still building.
